A top-down stealth game: a museum at night, one misfiled seal, and guards who know every footstep. Slip through three wings — the Gallery, the Archive, the Vault — past patrols with visible vision cones, hide behind display cases and shelves, watch the detection meter, and leave by the service door unseen. Guards patrol learnable routes, pause at waypoints, grow suspicious, and give chase when the alarm goes up; break line of sight and the alarm decays. Lantern pools expose you, shadow hides you, and procedural WebAudio footsteps tell you where a guard is. Vanilla JavaScript and Canvas 2D, no libraries, no network requests, no build step.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. WASD or arrows to move, Shift to sneak, mouse for menus, Esc to pause, M to mute.
